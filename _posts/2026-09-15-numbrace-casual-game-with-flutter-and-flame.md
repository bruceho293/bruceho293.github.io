## Objective
AS a developer, I always want to experience with game making since I enjoy games themselves and I love to figure out the technologies that uses to make game. During my university, after my first-year course about Mobile programming, I learnt how to use Java with Android Studio to make a usable android app. Even more, I attended a hackathon with friends and within 2 days, we managed to make a funtional fitness app when people can keep track of their activities at the gym.

With those knowledge and tutorial I found online (back in 2018, I don't have LLM to assist me on the coding progress), I made my first mobile game called Squarean (the Boring Man) and published it on Play Store. At that time, I got to know a lot about game mechanics as I studied and coded from scratch. The end result was very fun even though my game didn't attract a lot of interest from my friends.

From mobile games, I have tried more games on the web to see if the ideas I have works and people can actually enjoy playing them. You can probably see my other game on my other [post](/posts/hosting-static-files-with-git-modules/)

With Numbrace, I decided to use Flame and Flutter as it will help me bring my prototype to live through the web and maybe I would consider publishing it through mobile store so that people can download it on their phone to play it. 

I am still a beginner so any feedback about the game would be extremely helpful for me. You can send me your feedback through my email or social account like Linkedin.
